01 — Studio

Small studio. Full cycle.

We are a compact, developer-led studio. Every project is designed, written, coded and shipped in-house, so the person you talk to at the first consultation is the person who builds your game.

We focus on two things we know deeply: atmospheric story-driven games, and multiplayer games that run in a browser on any phone or laptop — no installs, just a link.

  • Narrative & mystery

    Point-and-click and hidden-object games with researched settings, layered stories and puzzles that respect the player.

  • Real-time multiplayer

    Server-authoritative browser games with rooms, invite links, reconnection and bots — built on Node.js and WebSockets.

  • Art & interface

    Art direction, UI and UX designed together with the code, so the game looks finished on every screen size.

What we can do for you

  • Game prototype in 2–4 weeks
  • Unity development (C#)
  • Browser & multiplayer games
  • Narrative & puzzle design
  • UI / UX for games
  • Deployment & support

File 002 In production

Case History

Mystery point-and-click · Hidden object · PC

Winter, the 1920s. A grand hotel high in the Alps hides its past as a sanatorium. Search the rooms, read the files nobody was meant to see, and find out what happened to the women who were sent here “for a rest”.

The story draws on real history: diagnoses of “hysteria” used to control women, and the shell shock that soldiers brought home from the First World War.

  • EngineUnity · C#
  • ScopeAct I · 30–40 minutes of play
  • LanguageEnglish
